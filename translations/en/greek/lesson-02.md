### Rules of accent

- An accent may stand on only one of the last three syllables.
- The acute accent (´) – a rise in pitch.
- The grave accent (`) – a fall in pitch (in connected speech).
- The circumflex accent (῀) – a rise followed by a fall (only on long vowels).

### Special rules

- In verbs the accent is placed as far from the end of the word as possible.
- In nouns and adjectives the accent stays on the same syllable as in the nominative singular.

### Enclitics and proclitics

- Enclitics are unstressed words that lean on the preceding word (for example, μου, σου).
- Proclitics are unstressed words that lean on the following word (for example, ὁ, ἡ, οἱ, αἱ, εἰς, ἐκ, ἐν, οὐ).
