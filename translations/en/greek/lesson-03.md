## 1. Grammatical categories of the verb

The Greek verb has the following categories:

- Tempus (tense) — praesens (present), aoristus (aorist), futurum (future), etc.
- Genus (voice) — activum (active), medium (middle), passivum (passive)
- Modus (mood) — indicativus (indicative), coniunctivus (subjunctive), optativus (optative), imperativus (imperative)
- Persona (person) — 1st, 2nd, 3rd
- Numerus (number) — singularis (singular), pluralis (plural)

## 2. Praesens indicativi activi (present indicative active)

Conjugation of the verb λύω (I untie):

| Person | Singular | Plural | Meaning, sg. | Meaning, pl. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | λύω | λύομεν | I untie | we untie |
| 2nd | λύεις | λύετε | you untie | you untie |
| 3rd | λύει | λύουσι(ν) | he unties | they untie |

## 3. Personal endings and connecting vowels

Verb stem: λυ-

Endings:

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | -ω | -ομεν |
| 2nd | -εις | -ετε |
| 3rd | -ει | -ουσι(ν) |

### Connecting vowels (thematische Vokale)

- before μ and ν — ο (λύομεν, λύουσι)
- before the others — ε (λύεις, λύετε, λύει)
- in the 1st person singular the ending -ω contains the connecting vowel ο + -μι (historically).

## 4. Peculiarities of the 3rd person plural

The ending -ουσι(ν) has a movable ν (ν ἐφελκυστικόν):

- -ουσι — before a consonant
- -ουσιν — before a vowel or at the end of a sentence
- Example: λύουσι τοὺς δούλους (before a consonant)
- Example: λύουσιν οἱ ἄνθρωποι (before a vowel)

## 6. Accent in verbs in -ω

Verbs take a recessive accent (the law of the three syllables):

- the accent falls on the third syllable from the end, if possible
- if the third syllable from the end is short, the accent moves to the second syllable
- Example: λύω, λύεις, λύει — the accent is on the stem
- Example: λαμβάνω, λαμβάνεις — the accent is on the stem

Exceptions: some verbs keep a fixed accent on the ending (for example, εἰμί — to be).
